Configure domain delegation

Creating a zone does not automatically delegate your domain. Configure delegation with every nameserver listed below so DNS queries reach the platform.

  • Whole domain (example.com): At your domain registrar, open the domain's nameserver settings and replace the existing nameservers with the nameservers below. These are the domain's NS records.
  • Subzone (cdn.example.com): In the parent domain's DNS settings at your registrar or current DNS provider, create an NS record for the corresponding subzone for each nameserver below. Use cdn as the record name if your provider appends example.com automatically, or cdn.example.com if it requires the full name. Do not change the parent domain's nameservers.
